03 — GUIDE

Temperature explained.

Understand the three temperature scales used by TempX.

°C

Celsius

Celsius is commonly used around the world for everyday temperature measurement.

0°C = Freezing
100°C = Boiling
°F

Fahrenheit

Fahrenheit is widely used for everyday temperature measurement in the United States.

32°F = Freezing
212°F = Boiling
K

Kelvin

Kelvin is an absolute temperature scale commonly used in scientific applications.

0 K = Absolute Zero
CONVERSION FORMULAS

How the conversion works

°C → °F (°C × 9/5) + 32
°F → °C (°F − 32) × 5/9
°C → K °C + 273.15
K → °C K − 273.15
